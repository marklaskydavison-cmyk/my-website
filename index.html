Howdy!

My Name is Mark Lasky-Davison. I am a 2020 graduate (Bachelor of Fine Arts – Honours) from the Victorian College of the Arts school of Film and Television. My films have been screened at festivals in Australia and overseas, including St Kilda Film Festival and London Australian Film Festival.

I am currently working on a script for my first feature film which will be shot in a slow cinema style and is set in the Victorian Countryside around several natural locations in the Murrindindi Shire. I also play drums for a Metal band named Dalliance; we are currently working on our third EP which will be accompanied by a series of music videos I am creating.

I believe movies are a fantastic tool for exploring the ‘human condition’ and furthering our self-awareness by relating to on-screen characters. They allow us to discuss deep topics with friends and family by giving everyone a common ground from which to spring ideas.
