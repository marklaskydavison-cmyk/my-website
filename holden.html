Starring:

Lauren McGuckin: “Holden” – Parked (2019), Premonition (2018), Sophies Turn (2017).

Albert Goikhman: “Father” – Deeper Than Yesterday (2010), Bronze (2011), The Novelist (2016).

Diana Stathis: “Rebecca”.


Holden is alone on her 18th birthday, stuck attending to her terminally ill mother's caravan park when she meets her father for the first time.

Director Statement

In Holden, I wanted to create an emotional piece that explores the complex boundaries between familial relationships. In particular, looking at the expectations we carry with us about certain people and the roles they should play in our lives. It doesn't always end up the way we hope.
