DALLIANCE

Members:

Vocals/Guitar: Jake Gonzalez Prudden

Vocals/Guitar: Luke Knowles

Vocals/Bass: Jackson Spessot

Drums: Mark Lasky-Davison

Formed in the Eastern Suburbs of Melbourne as a two piece in 2015 after the demise of their previous band. Jake Gonzalez Prudden and Mark Lasky-Davison began writing what would become "Its Sunrise" which was released in 2017. After another lineup change and the addition of bassist Jackson Spessot "Back Home" was released.

Finding their true sound as a four piece, and celebrating the band's 10th anniversary, they decided to scrap everything online and start fresh with the EP "Ten Years", featuring four favourite tracks re-recorded in the band's new style. Shortly after, the EP "Common Ground" was released, featuring six new songs.

2026 marks a new era for Dalliance.
