DALLIANCE

Members:

Vocals/Guitar: Jake Gonzalez Prudden

Bass: Jackson Spessot

Drums: Mark Lasky-Davison


Formed in the Eastern Suburbs of Melbourne as a two piece in 2015 after the demise of their previous band. Jake Gonzalez Prudden and Mark Lasky-Davison began writing what would become "Its Sunrise" which was released in 2017. After another lineup change and the addition of bassist Jackson Spessot "Back Home" has just been released.
